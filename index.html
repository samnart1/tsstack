General Information

This page serves as the central hub for information about Google Summer of Code 2026 at Open Data Hub. GSoC is a global program where Google sponsors contributors to work on open source projects over the summer, mentored by experienced developers from the community.

Open Data Hub is applying as a mentoring organization for GSoC 2026!

We are excited to welcome new contributors to work on innovative projects that help democratize access to open data for mobility, tourism, and sustainability in the Alpine region.

What is Open Data Hub?

Open Data Hub is a cross-border digital platform that aggregates data from multiple providers, making real-time information available for mobility, tourism, weather, and more through unified APIs.

Learn more

What is GSoC?

Google Summer of Code is a global program focused on bringing new contributors into open source. Contributors receive a stipend to work on a 12+ week project with guidance from mentors.

Learn more

Why Participate?

Gain real-world experience, contribute to meaningful open source projects, receive mentorship from industry professionals, and earn a stipend while building your portfolio.

Project Guidelines

Requirements for GSoC projects at Open Data Hub

Important Deadlines

The deadline to propose new project ideas is February 11, 2026. All projects must have confirmed mentors before this date.

1

Direct Relation to Open Data Hub

All proposed projects must directly relate to Open Data Hub and fit into the pre-existing work of our development teams. Projects should benefit our data platform, APIs, or ecosystem.

2

Two Mentors Required

All projects must have at least one primary mentor who is an active Open Data Hub contributor, plus a co-mentor for backup support. Mentors can only mentor one project each.

3

Qualification Task

Mentors are required to provide prospective contributors with a small task (1-3 hours) related to the proposed project. This helps both parties determine if they are a good match.

4

Open Source License

All code produced must be released under an approved open source license compatible with Open Data Hub's existing codebase (typically AGPL-2.0 or Apache-2.0).

For Mentors

How to mentor a project at Open Data Hub

If you are an experienced Open Data Hub contributor interested in mentoring, we would love to have you! Mentoring is a rewarding way to give back to the community and help develop the next generation of open source contributors.

Steps to Become a Mentor

1

Contact Us

Reach out on our communication channels to express your interest in mentoring.

2

Submit a Project Idea

Add your project idea to our Project Ideas page following the provided template. Include clear goals, required skills, and expected outcomes.

3

Read the Mentor Guide

Familiarize yourself with Google's GSoC Mentor Guide to understand your responsibilities and best practices.

For Contributors

How to participate as a GSoC contributor

Contributor Applications

GSoC contributor applications open March 24 - April 8, 2026. Start engaging with us early!

Getting Started

1

Check Eligibility

Ensure you meet the GSoC eligibility requirements: 18+ years old, beginner in open source, not restricted geographically.

2

Explore Our Projects

Browse the Project Ideas list and identify projects that match your skills and interests.

3

Join Our Community

Introduce yourself on our communication channels. Do not be shy, we are friendly! Ask questions, get to know mentors.

4

Make a Contribution

Start with a small contribution: fix a bug, improve documentation, or complete a qualification task. This demonstrates your commitment.

5

Write Your Proposal

Discuss your ideas with mentors and write a detailed proposal following our template. Submit early for feedback!

Proposal Tips

  • Submit early to get feedback from mentors
  • Be specific, vague proposals are rejected
  • Show your work, link to contributions you have made
  • Be realistic, do not overpromise
  • Communicate, discuss your ideas with mentors before submitting
